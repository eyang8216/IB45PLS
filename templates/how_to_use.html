❓ How to Use IB Study Hub

Your complete guide to mastering the platform

🚀 Getting Started

IB Study Hub is your all-in-one platform for IB Diploma preparation. Here's how to navigate and make the most of every feature:

  1. Choose a subject from the home page (Economics, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Math)
  2. Browse lessons in the subject index page
  3. Track progress using checkboxes and mastery badges
  4. Practice with AI-generated problems and flashcards
  5. Get help from the AI tutor anytime

📚 Subject Index Features

Each subject has a unified index page with powerful tracking tools:

✅
Completion Checkboxes
Check off lessons as you complete them. Your progress is automatically saved.
🎯
Mastery Badges
Click the badge to cycle through: Not Started → Learning → Proficient → Mastery. Track your understanding level for each lesson.
📊
Progress Stats
See completed lessons, remaining lessons, overall progress percentage, and your study streak at the top.
🔍
Search & Filter
Use the search box to find lessons by keyword. Filter by mastery level to focus on specific topics.
📁
Expand/Collapse Topics
Click topic headers to expand or collapse sections. Use "Expand All" / "Collapse All" buttons for quick navigation.

📖 Lesson Page Features

Inside each lesson, you'll find comprehensive content and interactive tools:

Core Content

  • Learning Objectives: Click to expand and see what you'll master
  • Detailed Explanations: Theory, examples, and step-by-step walkthroughs
  • Practice Problems: Built-in exercises with solutions
  • LaTeX Math: Properly formatted equations and formulas

Interactive Tools

🃏
Generate Flashcards
AI creates flashcards from the lesson content for quick review and memorization.
📝
Practice Problems
Generate custom practice questions based on the lesson topics. Get instant feedback.
🤖
Ask AI Tutor
Stuck? Click "Ask AI" to get help with concepts, ask questions, or request clarifications.

📊 Dashboard

Your personalized dashboard shows:

  • Overall Progress: Track completion across all subjects
  • Subject Breakdown: See which subjects need more attention
  • Recent Activity: View your latest completed lessons
  • Study Streak: Maintain your daily study momentum
💡 Tip: Visit your dashboard regularly to identify weak areas and plan your study sessions effectively.

📝 Language & Literature Papers

For Chinese and English Lang Lit courses:

  • Paper 1 Practice: Guided Literary Analysis with AI grading
  • Paper 2 Practice: Comparative Essay practice (Chinese only)
  • Rubric Feedback: Detailed criterion-by-criterion scoring
  • Improvement Suggestions: Personalized feedback to boost your grade

🎓 SAT Practice

Full-length SAT preparation with:

  • Timed Modules: Reading & Writing + Math sections with realistic timing
  • Auto-Grading: Instant score calculation
  • Adaptive Difficulty: Questions adjust to your performance
  • Score Reports: Track your progress over multiple attempts

💡 Study Tips

  1. Set Daily Goals: Complete 2-3 lessons per subject each day
  2. Use Mastery Badges: Don't move forward until you're "Proficient" or "Mastery"
  3. Practice Regularly: Use the flashcard and practice problem generators after each lesson
  4. Ask Questions: Use the AI tutor whenever you're confused—don't skip over unclear concepts
  5. Review Dashboard: Check your progress weekly to identify gaps
  6. Build Streaks: Study every day to maintain momentum and retain information

⌨️ Quick Tips

  • Use Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on Mac) to search within lessons
  • Bookmark frequently used subject index pages
  • Open multiple lessons in tabs to compare topics
  • Set browser notifications for study reminders
